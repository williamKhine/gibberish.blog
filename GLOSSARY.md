# Gibberish

Gibberish is a blogging community with open membership and reviewed access to publishing.

## Language

### Member

A person who has joined with a verified email. Members can follow writers, bookmark posts, react, and create private drafts.

### Writer

A member whose writer application was approved. Writers can publish, comment, upload media, and customize a public profile.

### Administrator

A designated member who can review writer applications and moderate the community. An administrator needs writer approval to publish.
